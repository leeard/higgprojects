My drill battery won’t charge

How to fix a drill battery that won’t charge

This one has the comments to prove it. People keep writing in to say it brought their dead battery back.

What I did

  1. If a battery has no charge at all, the charger acts like there’s no battery there.
  2. Connect it to a good, charged battery: negative to negative, positive to positive.
  3. Mine took about 10 seconds. Then I put it on the charger and the charger accepted it.

Use a matching battery from the same tool line, and stop right away if anything gets hot.
