My air compressor runs out

Boost a small air compressor with a propane tank

A lot of you found this one on your own. I needed more air for sandblasting the tractor, so I added a second tank.

What I did

  1. I teed in ahead of the regulator, so the second tank fills to full pressure.
  2. I picked a new propane tank that had never held propane, so there's no smell to deal with.
  3. I used a full flow fitting and Teflon tape. Watch out, propane threads are lefty tighty.
  4. Plug in the tank side of the hose first. A pressurized hose whips around, and I've got the cut on my leg to prove it.
  5. While you're out there, purge the water from your compressor tank. Mine ran and ran.

Be safe. This is not a how-to on making an old propane tank safe.
