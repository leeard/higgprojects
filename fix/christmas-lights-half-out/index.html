Half my lights are out

Half my Christmas tree wouldn’t light up. One LED was the problem.

I got into soldering with these little kits. I plugged this one in and half the tree stayed dark. It works in the end, and it’s a fun little craft night project if you want to learn to solder.

What I did

  1. Learn to read the resistor stripes before you start. Watch this part
  2. Then solder the parts: resistors, capacitors, transistors, and LEDs. Watch this part
  3. When half of it stays dark, go looking for the backwards LED. Mine was in the wrong way around. Watch this part
  4. If a part fights you coming back out, check your iron. Turns out I’d bumped mine down to 100. Watch this part

What I used

  • 3D Christmas tree soldering kit
  • TS100 soldering iron (now the upgraded TS101, power supply sold separately)
  • Magnetic helping hands
  • Solder sucker
  • Tip tinner
  • Kester 44 rosin core solder, 60/40 (the good stuff)
  • Blue silicone solder mat
